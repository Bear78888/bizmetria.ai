freelancer/agency market rates for comparable work and price 20% below.
Benchmarks below; each price ≈ market midpoint −20%, rounded to $x90/$x50.

Principles (owner-approved concept, DEC-031):
- Curated list only: we sell exclusively what we are confident we can build.
- The paid report keeps working as a self-serve instruction; buying a solution
  saves time, it is never required.
- AI matches recommendations to catalog items; **price and scope come from
  this catalog, never invented per-customer by the model.**
- Delivery for every item: working build + all accesses + step-by-step PDF
  + short screencast; 1 revision round and 14 days of fix-support included.
- Ongoing platform subscriptions (Make, Twilio, phone numbers, model usage)
  are the customer's own accounts and are stated on the order page — we never
  hide running costs inside a one-time price.
- Customer can optionally request a call; no mandatory calls.
